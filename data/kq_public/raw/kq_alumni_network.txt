Welcome to the KQ Alumni Network
KQ Reconnect: Celebrating Our Journey, Together
Your journey with Kenya Airways does not end when you leave the airline—it is simply the beginning of a lifelong connection to a family that values you. You are part of a powerful community of aviators, professionals, and leaders who have carried the Pride of Africa across the globe. Whether you are soaring in new careers, building businesses, pursuing passions, or exploring fresh opportunities, you never have to walk that path alone.
The Kenya Airways Alumni Network is here for you.
This is your space to reconnect, grow, and thrive alongside fellow alumni who share your story and ambitions.
