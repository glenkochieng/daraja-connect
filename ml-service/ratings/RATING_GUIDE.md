# Mentor–mentee relevance rating guide

Thank you for helping evaluate Daraja Connect. You will see pairs of profiles: one
**mentee** (a Kenya Airways Pride Centre graduate) and one possible **mentor** (a
current or former Kenya Airways employee). Rate how suitable the mentor would be for
this mentee.

You are not told how each mentor was chosen. Please rate every pair independently and
do not discuss ratings with other raters until everyone has finished.

## Scale

| Rating | Meaning | Typical signs |
|---|---|---|
| **2 – Highly relevant** | I would confidently recommend this mentor to this mentee. | The mentor has strong, hands-on expertise in the mentee's main goal, in the same or a closely related area, and is clearly senior enough to guide them. |
| **1 – Somewhat relevant** | A reasonable match, but not ideal. | The mentor covers some of the mentee's goals, or has the right general area without the specific expertise, or only limited experience in what the mentee most needs. |
| **0 – Not relevant** | I would not recommend this pairing. | Different area, the mentor's expertise does not address the mentee's goals, or the mentee would gain little. |

## Rules

1. Base the rating only on the information in the two profiles.
2. Judge suitability for **mentoring**, not whether the mentor is a good employee.
3. Ignore writing style, spelling and profile length unless they change what you understand about expertise or goals.
4. If a profile is too vague to judge, choose the rating you think is most likely and write "unclear" in the comment column.
5. Enter only `0`, `1` or `2` in the `rating` column. Do not edit other columns or reorder rows.
6. Save your file as `rater_<your initials>.csv` and return it to the researcher.

## Privacy

Rating sheets built from real profiles contain personal information. Keep them on the
device you were given, do not forward them, and delete your copy once you have returned it.
