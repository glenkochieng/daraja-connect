Kenya Airways Ground Services
Kenya Airways aims to be the leading ground handling agent in Kenya, offering exceptional services that support airlines in achieving operational excellence. With over a decade of ISAGO certification and extensive experience, our highly trained team ensures safety, efficiency, and passenger/cargo satisfaction for all types of aircraft.
We continuously invest in staff, cutting-edge technology, and modern ground support equipment to improve our services and maintain high standards. Our commitment to exceptional customer service allows us to build strong partnerships with airlines by addressing their unique needs with professionalism and care.

Kenya Airways’ Ground Support Equipment (GSE) is essential for smooth operations, from aircraft tugs to baggage carts. This modern equipment ensures timely departures and careful handling of luggage.
